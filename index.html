New Game

Everyone starts poor. First person to 20 points wins.

How to Play

  • Each turn, pick one move.
  • Work — Get 1 to 3 points. Sometimes the boss takes a cut.
  • Invest — Pay 2. You might get 4 to 6 back.
  • Strike — Try to raise pay for every worker.
  • Gamble — Big win or lose 2.
  • Hustle — Extra job. Often +2. Sometimes +5 or nothing.
  • Steal — Try to take 2 from the richest player.
  • First to 20 points wins.
